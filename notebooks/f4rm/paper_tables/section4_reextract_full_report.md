## §0 — Operational definitions (verbatim from spec)

- **`strict_dominates`** — every point on the other system's frontier is dominated by some point on F's frontier (`acc ≥ acc'` ∧ `mem ≤ mem'`, with one strict). Equivalently: **F is the only contributor to the joint Pareto envelope**.
- **`mixed`** — both systems contribute non-dominated points to the joint envelope.
- **`baseline_strict_dominates`** — symmetric (every F point dominated by some baseline point).
- **`infeasible`** — at least one of the two systems has no frontier points on this dataset.
- **`mean_pct_envelope_from_f4rm`** — averaged only over feasible datasets; the fraction of joint envelope points contributed by F4RM.

> **Note on `partial_dominates`:** Spec lists `f4rm_partial_dominates` and `baseline_partial_dominates` as separate bins, but their definitions overlap with `mixed` once `strict` is read literally. We collapse to `{strict_F, strict_B, mixed, infeasible}`. The `n_partial_*` columns in `section4_dominance_summary.csv` are 0 by design.
