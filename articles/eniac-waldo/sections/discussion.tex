\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The weightless network's advantage is a position on the deletion axis, not a new accuracy frontier. On static accuracy the random forest leads and the weightless network follows, and the same additive structure explains both: independent per-example counter updates make deletion a constant-time primitive, and they also cap capacity below a forest that splits on feature interactions. What the method offers is exact, millisecond, flat-memory deletion at competitive accuracy, a place the other exact methods reach only by spending seconds or gigabytes; the greedy DaRE forest reaches the same exactness at the accuracy-first end, so the two are complementary deployment priorities rather than competitors.

Several limitations bound these claims. The benchmark is synthetic, calibrated to real Where's-Waldo pages but abstracting each character to a structural trait, so transfer to photographed pages is untested, and the study covers one task family. The weightless network's bit-to-tuple mapping is unseeded, so its recognition accuracy fluctuates between about 0.88 and 0.92 across runs, and we report single-run values rather than multi-seed intervals. Its memory is competitive but not minimal, heavier than a plain forest, and its memory standing is an advantage only against the deletion-enabled DaRE forest, not against a forest that never has to forget. Membership inference is a single probe of forgetting, and ours is a confidence-threshold attack: for the weightless network the bit-identity certificate is the stronger guarantee, but it exists only because training is additive, and for the approximate baseline a stronger per-example attack, such as the likelihood-ratio attack~\cite{carlini2022lira} or its unlearning-specific variant~\cite{hayes2024ulira}, could surface residue our AUC does not.

The main threat to external validity is the gap between synthetic and real pages, but it bears on the benchmark, not the mechanism. Exactness follows from additive training, not from anything specific to Waldo: wherever a weightless recognizer is an appropriate model, deletion is the same counter subtraction, with the same certificate and the same cost. The result to carry forward is therefore narrow but portable, a weightless network that deletes exactly and cheaply by construction, established here on a controlled task before it is taken to messier ones.
