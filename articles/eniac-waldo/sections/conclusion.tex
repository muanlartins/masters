\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented weightless neural networks as an exact machine-unlearning substrate, together with a synthetic Where's-Waldo benchmark and an open evaluation harness that scores deletion as a first-class property. The experiments demonstrate that, because training is additive, deletion is a counter subtraction that is exact by construction and certifiable by direct comparison: across class erasure, poison removal, deletion streams, and backdoor scrubbing, the weightless network deleted in milliseconds at a flat memory budget with membership inference at chance, while the exact baselines reached the same exactness only at seconds-to-gigabyte cost and the approximate baseline left a measurable leak. One can therefore see that accuracy benchmarks, which score only what a model predicts and never what it can forget, keep a model family that forgets by construction invisible in the comparisons practitioners read; placing deletion on the same axis as accuracy makes that family legible and turns the right to be forgotten into a model-selection criterion, on which a weightless network is the latency-first exact option beside the accuracy-first forest.

Three directions follow. First, the accuracy gap to the random forest is the price of a low-capacity additive model, and closing it without surrendering exact deletion is an open problem: higher-capacity additive models, for instance ensembles of weightless discriminators that keep each update independent, could move the latency-first corner toward the forest's accuracy while leaving deletion a counter subtraction. Second, the same additive primitive that deletes on request also unlearns online: applied to each mistake as appearance drifts, it lets the model track change at microsecond cost, a continual-unlearning use we have only previewed here and that deserves a full prequential study. Third, because the mechanism is independent of the benchmark, the natural next test is transfer---training on the calibrated synthetic pages and deleting on real photographed ones---and, beyond Waldo, application wherever a weightless recognizer fits and forgetting is required.
